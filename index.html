    How to turn

    • Drag across a face to turn the row or column you grabbed. Let go and it snaps to the nearest quarter turn.
    • Drag the background to look around the cube.
    • Keys: U D L R F B M E S turn clockwise, add Shift for counter-clockwise.
    • The timer starts on your first turn and stops when the cube is solved.
